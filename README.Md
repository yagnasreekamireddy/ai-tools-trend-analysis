# AI Tools Interest Trend Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed market share, traffic, and growth trends of 6 major 
AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Perplexity, Grok) 
across 10 quarters from Q1 2024 to Q2 2026.

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Numpy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Data Sources
- SimilarWeb (web traffic)
- StatCounter (market share)
- First Page Sage (chatbot rankings)
- DataReportal (user growth)

## Key Findings
- ChatGPT lost 23pp market share (87% → 64%) in 2 years
- Gemini grew 4x faster (5% → 21%) powered by Google ecosystem
- Claude holds 3% traffic but 159% revenue growth ($850M → $2.2B)
- Grok recorded 9,300% YoY growth — fastest AI platform entry ever
- Copilot grew 1,038% in traffic yet holds only 5% market share
